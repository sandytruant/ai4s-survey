Paradigm (a): Neural Network Wavefunctions (VMC)

FermiNet & Psiformer

Solving the many-electron Schrödinger equation directly via Variational Monte Carlo (VMC) with antisymmetric neural networks, Forward Laplacian autodiff, and KFAC natural gradients.

Verified Sources: FermiNet Paper (Phys. Rev. Research 2020) | Psiformer Paper (ICLR 2023) | GitHub: google-deepmind/ferminet | GitHub: microsoft/folx

2.2.1 Overall Workflow: AI vs. Non-AI Kernels

FermiNet (multi-stream equivariant MLP) and Psiformer (self-attention Transformer over electron coordinates) replace classical hand-crafted Slater-Jastrow wavefunctions with a deep neural ansatz $\Psi_\theta(\mathbf{r}_1,\dots,\mathbf{r}_N)$ trained end-to-end by minimizing the variational energy $E[\Psi_\theta] = \langle \Psi_\theta | \hat{H} | \Psi_\theta \rangle / \langle \Psi_\theta | \Psi_\theta \rangle$.

Figure 2.2: End-to-end VMC training loop in FermiNet/Psiformer. Unlike standard ML inference, evaluating the physics loss requires computing the exact second-order Laplacian $\nabla^2 \log|\Psi_\theta|$ at every walker step.

2.2.2 Computational Profiling & Compiler Bottlenecks

Metric / Aspect Observed Behavior & Published Profiling
Hardware & Framework JAX + XLA + kfac_jax on NVIDIA V100 / A100 GPUs (standard batch size 4,096 MCMC walkers; FP32/TF32 for network, FP32/FP64 stability for determinants).
Primary Bottleneck: Laplacian ($\nabla^2 \Psi$) Standard reverse-mode nested autodiff (computing $\text{tr}(\mathbf{H}_{\mathbf{r}})$ via jax.jvp over jax.grad) scales poorly in both compute and activation memory ($O(N^2)$ to $O(N^3)$ with electron count $N$).
Forward Laplacian (folx) Speedup Li et al. (2023, Forward Laplacian, microsoft/folx) replaces nested AD with sparse forward-mode propagation, exploiting block-diagonal structure in electron-coordinate Jacobians to achieve >10× speedup per VMC iteration on A100 GPUs for FermiNet/Psiformer on extended molecules.
Optimizer Bottleneck (KFAC) First-order Adam fails to converge to chemical accuracy. KFAC-JAX requires computing Kronecker-factored Fisher matrices and inverting them per layer, adding memory footprint and small-matrix Cholesky/eigh overhead.
Architectural Scaling Psiformer's self-attention scales as $O(N^2)$ in electron count rather than FermiNet's dense pairwise 2-electron stream, improving expressiveness on larger systems (up to ~80–100 electrons) while remaining bounded by determinant $O(N^3)$ and Laplacian cost.

2.2.3 Dedicated Accelerators & Kernel Libraries

  • folx (Forward Laplacian for JAX): Compiler-level AD transformation (microsoft/folx) that fuses Laplacian propagation and prunes zero off-diagonal Jacobian blocks, turning an activation-memory-bound kernel into a compute-efficient forward pass on NVIDIA A100/H100.
  • JAX / XLA & Google TPU / NVIDIA Tensor Cores: DeepMind's ferminet leverages XLA whole-program compilation to fuse elementwise activations with dense GEMMs (attention projections in Psiformer) and batched slogdet kernels across 4,096 walkers.
  • No Custom ASICs Yet: No dedicated silicon ASIC exists for NN-VMC; workloads run on general-purpose AI GPUs (A100/H100) and TPUs, where high FP32 Tensor Core throughput + large HBM capacity for intermediate AD tapes are the decisive hardware features.
