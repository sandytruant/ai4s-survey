1.2 Classical Methods & Computational Bottlenecks

Dissecting the Self-Consistent Field (SCF) loop, plane-wave vs. localized basis sets, $O(N^3)$ dense eigensolvers, 3D FFT all-to-all communication, and CCSD(T).

The Classical Method Hierarchy (Accuracy vs. Scaling)

Classical electronic structure methods trade off physical accuracy against polynomial (or exponential) compute complexity in the number of electrons/basis functions $N$:

Method Scaling Core Computational Kernels Typical System Limit
Kohn-Sham DFT (GGA / meta-GGA) $O(N^3)$ Iterative SCF loop: 3D FFTs + $H\psi$ matmuls + dense diagonalization / orthogonalization 100 – 2,000 atoms
Hybrid DFT (e.g., B3LYP, HSE06) $O(N^3 \text{–} N^4)$ Standard DFT + 4-center Electron Repulsion Integrals (ERIs) / exact exchange ($10\text{–}50\times$ slower) 50 – 500 atoms
Hartree-Fock (HF) $O(N^3 \text{–} N^4)$ Fock matrix construction (4-index ERI contraction) + Roothaan-Hall generalized eigensolve $FC = SCE$ 100 – 1,000 atoms
CCSD(T) ("Gold Standard") $O(N^7)$ High-dimensional tensor contractions (6D/4D amplitudes) with massive memory & I/O bandwidth demand 10 – 40 atoms
Full CI (Exact Diagonalization) $O(e^N)$ Sparse Hamiltonian matrix-vector products in combinatorially growing determinant space ≤ 20 electrons

Inside the Kohn-Sham SCF Loop: Dataflow & Bottlenecks

Every geometry step requires converging the Self-Consistent Field (SCF) loop—typically 10 to 50 iterations (or 100+ for metallic/magnetic systems with charge sloshing)—until the input density $\rho_{\text{in}}$ matches the output density $\rho_{\text{out}}$ within $\sim 10^{-6}$ Ha.

Basis Set Trade-offs: Plane Waves vs. Localized Orbitals

Plane-Wave Basis (VASP, Quantum ESPRESSO)

  • Representation: $\psi_i(\mathbf{r}) = \sum_{\mathbf{G}} c_{i,\mathbf{G}} e^{i\mathbf{G}\cdot\mathbf{r}}$ ($N_{\text{PW}} \sim 10^4\text{–}10^6$ coefficients per band).
  • Kernels: Iterative eigensolvers (Davidson / RMM-DIIS / LOBPCG) repeatedly apply $H\psi_i$. Kinetic energy is diagonal in reciprocal ($\mathbf{G}$) space; local potential $V_{\text{eff}}\psi_i$ is diagonal in real ($\mathbf{r}$) space.
  • Hardware Bottleneck: Switching between spaces requires batched 3D FFTs, which trigger global MPI_Alltoall communication across nodes and saturate memory/network bisection bandwidth.

Localized Atomic Orbitals (PySCF, SIESTA, OpenMX, FHI-aims)

  • Representation: $\psi_i(\mathbf{r}) = \sum_{\mu} C_{\mu i} \phi_\mu(\mathbf{r})$ ($N_{\text{AO}} \sim 10\text{–}50$ basis functions per atom).
  • Kernels: Builds sparse/block-sparse Hamiltonian $H_{\mu\nu}$ and overlap $S_{\mu\nu}$ matrices, then solves the generalized eigenvalue problem $HC = SCE$ via Cholesky factorization + tridiagonalization (ScaLAPACK / ELPA / cuSOLVER).
  • Hardware Bottleneck: Householder tridiagonalization is dominated by BLAS-2 (matrix-vector) operations with poor cache reuse and synchronization barriers at every panel step; requires strict FP64 precision.
