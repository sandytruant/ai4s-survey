AI for Science (AI4S): An Accelerator Architecture & Compiler Survey

A visual-first, systems-level survey of the three dominant computational pillars of AI4S—Electronic Structure, Molecular Dynamics, and Continuum Mechanics PDEs—mapping physical equations to data structures, Amdahl's Law boundaries, profiling bottlenecks, and dedicated hardware.

1. Multi-Scale Physical & Computational Coupling

Scientific simulation spans orders of magnitude in space and time. Outputs from finer-scale quantum models parameterize atomic force fields, which in turn yield constitutive material properties (viscosity, thermal conductivity, elastic moduli) for macroscopic continuum PDEs. For hardware architects and compiler engineers, each scale exhibits distinct tensor shapes, memory access patterns, and AI-vs-classical bottlenecks.

Cross-scale physical coupling and the shift in computational primitives from classical HPC kernels to AI4S workloads.
