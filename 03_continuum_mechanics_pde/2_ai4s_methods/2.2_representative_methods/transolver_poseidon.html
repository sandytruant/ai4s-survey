Transolver & Poseidon: PDE Transformers & Foundation Models

Linear-complexity Physics-Attention on arbitrary meshes (Transolver) and multiscale pretrained operator transformers (Poseidon).

Physics-Attention / Foundation Model Transolver (arXiv:2402.02366) • GitHub: thuml/Transolver • Poseidon (arXiv:2405.19101) • GitHub: camlab-ethz/poseidon

2.2.1 Overall Workflow

Standard Vision/Graph Transformers suffer from $O(N^2)$ attention complexity on $10^5$–$10^6$-cell industrial meshes. Transolver (Wu et al., ICML 2024 Spotlight) solves this via Physics-Attention: it softly assigns $N$ mesh points into $M \ll N$ learnable physical-state slices (e.g., boundary layer, wake, freestream), runs $O(M^2)$ self-attention among slices, and deslices back to mesh points in $O(N)$ linear time. Poseidon (Herde et al., NeurIPS 2024) scales a multiscale operator transformer (scOT) with time-conditioned layer norms across 21M–629M parameters for zero/few-shot PDE transfer.

Figure 1: Transolver Physics-Attention. By compressing $N$ mesh points into $M$ physical-state slices, irregular graph message passing is replaced by dense Tensor-Core-friendly matrix multiplications.
