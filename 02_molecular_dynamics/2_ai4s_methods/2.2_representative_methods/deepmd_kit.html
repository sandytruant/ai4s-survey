DeePMD-kit (Deep Potential Molecular Dynamics)

Invariant local-environment descriptors + compact MLPs engineered for leadership-class supercomputers (100M+ atoms on Summit, 149 ns/day on Fugaku).

Invariant Descriptor + MLP CUDA / ROCm / SVE / NPU  |  GitHub: deepmodeling/deepmd-kit  |  Summit Gordon Bell Paper (arXiv:2005.00223)  |  Fugaku SC24 Paper (arXiv:2409.06954)

2.2.1 Overall Workflow: AI vs. Non-AI Pipeline

DeePMD-kit (using the DeepPot-SE smooth edition) avoids expensive spherical harmonics and Clebsch-Gordan tensor products. Instead, it constructs a symmetry-preserving local environment matrix $\tilde{\mathcal{R}}_i$ from relative neighbor coordinates within $r_c$, passes radial distances through an embedding MLP, contracts into an invariant descriptor $\mathcal{D}_i$, and evaluates a fitting MLP to produce per-atom energy $E_i$.

Key Advantage: Strictly local (single cutoff r_c, no multi-hop GNN message passing) → minimal MPI ghost-atom halo volume!

Architectural Bottleneck: Profiling shows >90% of runtime is spent in DNN inference (embedding net + descriptor contraction). At extreme strong scaling, ghost-atom halo communication across $r_c$ sets the lower bound on step latency, while TensorFlow/PyTorch dispatch overhead must be stripped out via C++/CUDA/ROCm custom operators.

2.2.3 Dedicated Accelerators & Hardware Co-Design

  • Classical MD ASICs (Baseline Comparison): D. E. Shaw Anton 1 / 2 / 3 (512-node Anton 3 achieves >100 $\mu$s/day on 1M atoms) and RIKEN MDGRAPE-4 / 4A (custom SoC with non-bonded pipelines + 3D torus) accelerate fixed empirical force fields, not neural networks.
  • DeePMD-kit Custom Backends: Dedicated optimizations exist across NVIDIA V100/A100 Tensor Cores (mixed FP16/FP32), AMD ROCm (Frontier), ARM SVE (Fugaku A64FX), Sunway TaihuLight/OceanLight many-core architectures, and model compression / polynomial tabulation (replacing embedding MLP inference with cubic spline lookup tables in L1/shared memory).
