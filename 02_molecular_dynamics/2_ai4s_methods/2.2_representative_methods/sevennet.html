SevenNet (+ FlashTP): Multi-GPU Spatial Decomposition & Fused Tensor Products

Scaling multi-layer equivariant GNNs across LAMMPS GPU clusters (>80% weak scaling) and accelerating Clebsch-Gordan kernels 41.6× with FlashTP.

2.2.1 Overall Workflow: Layer-by-Layer Halo Exchange in LAMMPS

Unlike Allegro (which drops message passing), SevenNet retains NequIP's multi-layer equivariant message passing for high accuracy while solving the multi-GPU scaling problem in LAMMPS. Instead of fetching an $L \cdot r_c$ ghost halo all at once, SevenNet performs per-layer MPI halo communication of node features across a single-$r_c$ boundary between message-passing layers.

Trade-off: Avoids O((L·r_c)³) memory explosion per GPU in exchange for L lightweight MPI point-to-point exchanges per step.

2.2.2 Computational Profiling & Bottlenecks

Multi-GPU LAMMPS Scaling
(32-GPU cluster, $\text{SiO}_2$)		 >80% weak-scaling parallel efficiency; near-ideal strong scaling when GPUs are saturated Avoids redundant computation on overlapping $L\cdot r_c$ halos; communication scales with surface area of subdomain.

(FlashTP vs. e3nn / cuEquivariance)		 Kernel-level: up to 41.6× faster than e3nn and 60.8× faster than cuEquivariance CG tensor products consume 75%–90% of runtime & memory in equivariant MLIPs due to sparse structure & intermediate edge tensors.
End-to-End Model
(SevenNet-l3i5 + FlashTP)		 4.2× inference speedup; 3.5× training speedup; >6× peak memory reduction Compile-time path aggregation, sparsity-aware CG skipping, and kernel fusion keep intermediates in GPU registers/shared memory.

2.2.3 Dedicated Accelerators & Kernel Libraries

  • FlashTP (SNU-ARC/flashTP): Open-source C++/CUDA library implementing fused, sparsity-aware, path-aggregated Clebsch-Gordan tensor products for SevenNet, MACE, and NequIP.
  • cuEquivariance & LAMMPS-MPI-CUDA: Native multi-GPU spatial decomposition in LAMMPS with CUDA-aware MPI for direct GPU-to-GPU halo transfers.
